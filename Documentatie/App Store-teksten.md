## 2.0.2 — releaseopmerkingen (Engels)

• Fixed: restoring a meal from your history removed it for the person you share
  the list with. The other way round, a meal you ticked off sometimes stayed on
  their list.
• After updating, your list is reconciled with iCloud once, so you both see
  exactly the same list again.
• A change that iCloud rejects is now retried instead of quietly being left
  behind.
• Changes made by the other person now reach your widget and Apple Watch even
  when you haven't opened the app for a while.

## Beschrijving (Engels)

What's on the menu tonight?

Whether you want to cook more organised every week, let your kids vote on their
favourite dishes, or simply avoid a bit of chaos, "What's for Dinner" gives you
the perfect overview. Free. No ads. No hidden costs. Just you and your meals.

PLAN TOGETHER
Invite your partner or housemate and you both work in the same list. When one of
you adds something, the other sees it right away. Your list stays in your own
iCloud: we run no servers and never see your meals.

ALWAYS WITHIN REACH
• A home screen widget showing what you are eating today and tomorrow.
• An Apple Watch app and complications, so it is right there on your wrist.
• The list moves on by itself at midnight — even if you have not opened the app
  all week.

SIMPLE AND SMART
• Drag your meals into whatever order you like.
• Days or numbers next to your meals, whichever suits you.
• Emoji magic: hundreds of dishes are recognised automatically, in Dutch,
  English, German, French and Spanish. On devices with Apple Intelligence your
  iPhone works out an emoji for dishes the list does not know yet — and that
  happens entirely on your device.
• A history of everything you have eaten, restored with a single swipe.
